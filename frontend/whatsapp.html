What the citizen sees

No app to install. The resident sends a photo and a location pin to one WhatsApp number. The verification step runs before anything reaches an official.

  1. The photo and the location pin arrive as two messages, in either order. The server keeps a short session per phone number until it has both.
  2. YOLOv8 classifies the blockage. Below 0.70 confidence, the bot asks for a clearer photo instead of raising a ticket.
  3. PostGIS finds which agency owns that channel (GCC, WRD or Forest Dept) and routes the ticket.
  4. The blockage updates the channel's roughness and flow area in the twin, so the command centre recalculates immediately.

This page talks to the same server and pipeline as the real WhatsApp number. Sample photos use fixed classifier scores; real photos sent to WhatsApp use your YOLOv8 weights, or go to manual review on the dashboard if none are configured.
