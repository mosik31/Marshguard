MarshGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

MarshGuard is a student project from St. Joseph's College of Engineering, Chennai. It lets residents of the Velachery – Pallikaranai – Okkiyam area report blocked drains and canals on WhatsApp, so the report can be checked and passed to the department that maintains that drain. It is a prototype, not an official government service.

What we collect

Only what you send to the MarshGuard WhatsApp number:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Photos you send of drains or canals.
  • The location you share, used only to find the nearest drain.
  • Messages you type, such as STATUS MG-3100.

We do not collect your contacts, other chats, or anything else from your phone.

How we use it

  • To check whether the photo shows a blockage and to match your location to a drain.
  • To create a ticket and route it to the responsible department (for example GCC, WRD or the Forest Department).
  • To send you updates about your report on WhatsApp.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share your phone number with anyone. Officials using the MarshGuard dashboard see only the last four digits of your number.

Who processes it

Messages travel through WhatsApp, operated by Meta, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. The MarshGuard server runs on Render (render.com).

How long we keep it

This is a prototype. Reports, photos and phone numbers are stored only on the demo server and are deleted when the server restarts or the demo ends, and in any case within 90 days.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted at any time, email team-email@example.com with the subject "Delete my MarshGuard data" and the WhatsApp number you used. We delete your reports, photos and number within 7 days and confirm by email. You can also stop at any time by simply not messaging the number again.
